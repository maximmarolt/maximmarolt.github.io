# JARVIS Video Publisher

Welcome to JARVIS Video Publisher.

JARVIS is a personal AI-powered application that creates original short-form videos and helps manage publishing and analytics for accounts authorized by its owner.

## What JARVIS Does

- Creates original fictional stories and videos.
- Generates AI narration and subtitles.
- Manages authorized YouTube publishing.
- Retrieves YouTube channel analytics.
- Helps improve future videos using performance data.

## Google Account Access

JARVIS uses Google's official OAuth authorization system to access the YouTube accounts its owner authorizes.

OAuth credentials are stored locally with restricted file permissions. The owner can revoke access through their Google Account settings.
