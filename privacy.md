# JARVIS Video Publisher — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 22, 2026**

## Overview

JARVIS Video Publisher is a personal AI-powered application for creating original videos, managing authorized YouTube uploads, and analyzing video performance.

## Information We Access

When authorized through Google OAuth, JARVIS may access:

- YouTube channel identity and information.
- Video information and publication status.
- YouTube Analytics data, including views and engagement statistics.
- Permissions needed to upload and manage videos.

## How Information Is Used

This information is used to verify account ownership, manage authorized publishing, prevent duplicate uploads, track performance, and improve future videos.

## Data Storage and Security

JARVIS stores OAuth credentials and operational data locally on the owner's computer. Authentication files use restricted access permissions.

JARVIS does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising targeting. Information is shared with Google APIs only as necessary for the application's authorized functions.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Local application records are retained until the owner deletes them. The owner can request local data deletion and revoke Google access through their Google Account permissions.

Revoking access prevents further authorized API access but does not automatically erase previously stored local records.

## User Control

The owner controls account connections, publishing authorization, and application operation.
